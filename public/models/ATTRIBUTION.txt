  STL mirror used: https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D

Changes made (scripts/build-models.mjs):
  - converted STL to glTF (metres, Y-up), merged structures into named groups
  - simplified meshes, recomputed normals, quantized and meshopt-compressed
  - split the skin surface into body regions; the groin area is flattened and
    shown as swim shorts for a child-appropriate presentation
  - reproductive structures were excluded
